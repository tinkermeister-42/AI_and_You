\qquad\qquad
\textbf{Filter:}\quad
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|}
\hline

$-1$ & 0 & 1 \\ \hline
$-1$ & 0 & 1 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\vspace{0.5em}
Put the filter over 3 columns of the picture. Multiply each pair of numbers, then add all 9.

\vspace{0.4em}
\begin{tabular}{|R{1.9in}|R{3.2in}|c|}
\hline
\textbf{Filter over columns} & \textbf{Math} & \textbf{Answer} \\ \hline
1, 2, 3 & \ans{each row: $-0 + 0 + 0 = 0$} & \ans{0} \\ \hline
2, 3, 4 & \ans{each row: $-0 + 0 + 9 = 9$, so $9 + 9 + 9$} & \ans{27} \\ \hline
3, 4, 5 & \ans{each row: $-0 + 0 + 9 = 9$} & \ans{27} \\ \hline
4, 5, 6 & \ans{each row: $-9 + 0 + 9 = 0$} & \ans{0} \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.7em}
Where are the big answers? \ansline{4.6in}{Where the picture changes from 0 to 9: the edge.}
