{\large\textbf{2. Stronger connections.}}

\vspace{0.4em}
When you practice, the synapses between your neurons get \ansline{1.3in}{stronger}.

\vspace{0.8em}
``Neurons that fire together, \ansline{0.9in}{wire} together.''

\vspace{1.0em}
{\large\textbf{3. Pavlov's dogs.}}

\vspace{0.4em}
Pavlov's team played a \ansline{1.6in}{sound (a metronome)} right before they gave the dogs \ansline{0.8in}{food}.

\vspace{0.8em}
Soon the dogs drooled when they heard the sound, even with no \ansline{0.8in}{food}.

\vspace{0.8em}
Learning to connect two things that happen together is called \ansline{1.4in}{conditioning}.

\vspace{0.8em}
In AI, the strength of a connection is a number called a \ansline{1.1in}{weight}.
