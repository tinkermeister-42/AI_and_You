{\large\textbf{2. Multiply, add, compare.}}

\vspace{0.4em}
An artificial neuron \ansline{1.1in}{multiplies} each input by its weight, \ansline{0.8in}{adds} them up, and \ansline{1.1in}{compares} the total with the threshold.

\vspace{0.9em}
Inputs: 1, 0, 1. \quad Weights: 3, 2, $-1$. \quad Threshold: 2.

\vspace{0.5em}
Total $= (1 \times 3) + (0 \times 2) + (1 \times -1) =$ \ansline{0.9in}{$2$} \quad Does it fire (2 or more)? \ansline{0.8in}{yes}

\vspace{0.9em}
Inputs: 1, 1, 1. \quad Weights: 1, $-2$, $-1$. \quad Threshold: 2.

\vspace{0.5em}
Total $=$ \ansline{2.4in}{$1 - 2 - 1 = -2$} \quad Does it fire? \ansline{0.8in}{no}
