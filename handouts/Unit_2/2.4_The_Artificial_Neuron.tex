\vspace{1.0em}
{\large\textbf{2. Multiply, add, compare.}}

\vspace{0.4em}
An artificial neuron \ansline{1.1in}{multiplies} each input by its weight, \ansline{0.8in}{adds} them up, and \ansline{1.1in}{compares} the total with the threshold.

\vspace{0.8em}
Strength 5, Magic 2. \quad Weights: Strength 1, Magic $-1$. \quad Threshold: 0. \quad More than the threshold: Fighter.

\vspace{0.5em}
Total $= (5 \times 1) + (2 \times -1) =$ \ansline{1.0in}{$5 - 2 = 3$} \quad Fighter or Wizard? \ansline{0.9in}{Fighter}

\vspace{0.8em}
Strength 2, Magic 3. \quad Weights: Strength 2, Magic $-1$. \quad Threshold: 2.

\vspace{0.5em}
Total $=$ \ansline{2.2in}{$(2 \times 2) + (3 \times -1) = 4 - 3 = 1$} \quad Fighter or Wizard? \ansline{0.9in}{Wizard}
