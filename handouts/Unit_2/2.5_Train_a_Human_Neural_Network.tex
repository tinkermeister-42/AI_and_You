\partner

\textbf{The club:} Fighters and Wizards can join. Giants and Minis can't. \quad \textbf{Strong} = 4 or 5 stars = \textbf{1}. Not strong = \textbf{0}.

\vspace{0.2em}
\textbf{Fire:} a neuron stands up if its total is \textbf{more than} its threshold.

\vspace{0.2em}
\textbf{Fix rule:} Should have fired: \textbf{ADD} 1 to each weight whose input was 1, threshold $-1$. \quad Fired but shouldn't: \textbf{SUBTRACT}.

\vspace{0.2em}
\textbf{Who's to Blame?} Find the hidden neuron closest to its threshold. Would the answer be right if it changed? Yes: it fixes itself. No: the output fixes itself.
