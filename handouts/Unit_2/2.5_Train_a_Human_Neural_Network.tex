\textbf{The rule.} total $= (A \times \text{Attack weight}) + (S \times \text{Special weight}) + \text{bias}$. \quad More than 0: \textbf{FIGHTING}. \quad 0 or less: \textbf{PSYCHIC}.

\vspace{0.3em}
\textbf{The fix rule.} Right: no change. \quad Said P, really F: \textbf{ADD} A, S, 1. \quad Said F, really P: \textbf{SUBTRACT} A, S, 1.
