{\large\textbf{2. How detectors guess.}}

\vspace{0.5em}
A chatbot picks likely next words, so AI writing is very \ansline{1.4in}{predictable.}

\vspace{0.7em}
A detector says ``AI'' when the words are \ansline{2.2in}{very predictable.}

\vspace{0.7em}
Human writing that's predictable: \ansline{4.4in}{short sentences and common words,}

\vspace{0.5em}
\ansline{6.9in}{like many people writing in a second language.}

\vspace{0.7em}
When a detector says ``AI'' but a person wrote it, that's a \ansline{1.5in}{false positive.}
