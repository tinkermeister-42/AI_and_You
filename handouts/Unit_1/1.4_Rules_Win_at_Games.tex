{\large\textbf{3. Deep Blue.}}

\vspace{0.4em}
In \ansline{0.8in}{1997}, IBM's Deep Blue beat the world chess champion, \ansline{1.8in}{Garry Kasparov}.

\vspace{0.9em}
Deep Blue checked about \ansline{1.4in}{200 million} chess positions every second.

\vspace{0.9em}
Did Deep Blue learn from examples? \ansline{3.4in}{No. It used rules that people wrote, and speed.}
