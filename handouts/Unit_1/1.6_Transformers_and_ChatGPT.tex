{\large\textbf{2. Attention.}}

\begin{center}\resizebox{0.85\linewidth}{!}{\fig{Unit_1/1.6_attention}}\end{center}

A transformer looks at every word. For each word, it decides which other words \ansline{1.5in}{matter most}.

\vspace{0.8em}
This is called \ansline{1.3in}{attention}. Google scientists shared the transformer in \ansline{0.8in}{2017}.

{\large\textbf{3. GPT and ChatGPT.}}

\vspace{0.5em}
\begin{tabular}{@{}c@{\ \ =\ \ }ll}
\textbf{\Large G} & \ansline{1.4in}{Generative} & it makes new text \\[0.5em]
\textbf{\Large P} & \ansline{1.4in}{Pre-trained} & it learned first from a huge amount of text \\[0.5em]
\textbf{\Large T} & \ansline{1.4in}{Transformer} & it uses attention to connect the words \\
\end{tabular}

\vspace{0.8em}
A chatbot writes by predicting the \ansline{1.3in}{next word}, again and again.

\vspace{0.8em}
ChatGPT came out in \ansline{1.4in}{November 2022}. About \ansline{1.2in}{100 million} people used it in about 2 months.

\vspace{0.8em}
In 2024, two \ansline{1.3in}{Nobel Prizes} went to scientists who worked on AI.
