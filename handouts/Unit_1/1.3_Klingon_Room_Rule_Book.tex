{\Large\bfseries The Klingon Room: Rule Book\ifkey\ \textcolor{keyred}{KEY}\fi}\par\vspace{0.6em}

\textbf{For the person in the room.} A note comes in. Find the note that looks \textbf{exactly} the same in the left column. Big and small letters matter! Copy the reply from the right column onto the back of the note. Pass it out.

\vspace{0.3em}
You do not need to know what the notes say. Just match the shapes.

\textbf{Note cards, for the people outside the room.} Cut out the cards. Pass one note into the room. When the reply comes back, check it with the meaning on your card. Does the reply make sense?
