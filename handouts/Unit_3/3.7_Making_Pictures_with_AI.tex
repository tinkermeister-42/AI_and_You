{\large\textbf{2. From noise to picture.}}

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Training:} the computer adds \ansline{1.2in}{noise} to millions of pictures. A neural network practices guessing

\vspace{0.5em}
\ansline{3.4in}{which part of the picture is noise.}

\vspace{0.7em}
\textbf{Making a picture:} the AI starts with pure \ansline{1.0in}{noise.} It removes \ansline{2.2in}{a little noise at a time,}

\vspace{0.5em}
again and again. My \ansline{1.0in}{words} guide every guess. This is called \ansline{1.3in}{diffusion.}
