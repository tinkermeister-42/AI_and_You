\vspace{1.0em}
{\large\textbf{2. Why does a chatbot make things up?}}

\vspace{0.5em}
A chatbot predicts \ansline{1.8in}{likely words.} It does not \ansline{2.5in}{look up each fact.}

\vspace{0.7em}
When AI writes something false as if it were true, it is called a \ansline{1.7in}{hallucination}.

\vspace{1.0em}
