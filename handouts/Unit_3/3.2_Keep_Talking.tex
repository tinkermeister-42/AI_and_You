{\large\textbf{4. The new chat test.}} Ask: ``What topic did I ask about?''

\vspace{0.6em}
In the \textbf{same} chat, it said: \ansline{4.5in}{Answers vary. It named my topic.}

\vspace{0.8em}

\vspace{0.8em}

\vspace{1.1em}
{\large\textbf{5. Careful when you push back.}}

\vspace{0.5em}
If I type ``Are you sure? I think that is wrong,'' a chatbot may \ansline{2.4in}{change a right answer.}

\vspace{0.8em}
Why? \ansline{6.3in}{It predicts what comes next. An answer that agrees with me is a likely next answer.}

\vspace{0.8em}
To find out if an answer is true, I should \ansline{3.6in}{check a real source.}
